## RECORDING NOTES

### If using Canva AI Voice:
- Choose a **professional male voice** (e.g., "Marcus" or "Daniel")
- Set speed to **1.0x** (normal)
- Paste the narration text above
- The AI voice will read it in ~25-29 seconds

### If recording yourself:
- Speak at a **moderate pace** — these are short sentences
- Pause briefly between sections (matches the visual cuts)
- Emphasize key words: **"invisible," "PayPal," "Groq," "Africa"**
- The talking head footage should show you speaking these lines

### Visual timing in Canva:
- **0:00–0:03**: Title card with GigSure AI logo + green shield (static)
- **0:03–0:26**: Your talking head footage (blue shirt, window background)
- **0:26–0:29**: Return to GigSure AI logo for the tagline
- Add the green shield as a **small overlay/watermark** in the corner during the talking head section
- Keep text overlays minimal — the narration carries the message

### Music (optional):
- Add a subtle, professional background track
- Keep it low volume (10-15%) so the voiceover is clear
- Use Canva's "Add audio" feature — search for "corporate" or "tech" background music
